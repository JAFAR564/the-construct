# Faction Profile: Ironborn Collective

**Motto:** *"No code. No magic. Just steel and will."*

## 1. Core Philosophy
The Ironborn Collective represents the brutal reality of survival in the lawless sectors of the grid. They are a loose confederation of survivors forged in the fire of countless conflicts. Where the Technocrats rely on code and the Keepers of the Veil rely on ancient rites, the Ironborn trust only what they can build with their own hands, hold, and use to defend themselves. They view complex systems—be it advanced AI or arcane magic—with extreme suspicion, believing that true strength comes from self-reliance, physical prowess, and unyielding willpower.

## 2. Organization & Structure
The Collective is highly decentralized, operating as a network of heavily fortified strongholds, scrap-yards, and mobile caravans. 
- **Warlords/Chieftains:** Each settlement is led by the strongest or most cunning survivor, who dictates the laws of their domain.
- **Scavengers:** The lifeblood of the Collective, venturing into the most dangerous, anomaly-ridden sectors to recover raw materials, scrap metal, and salvageable technology.
- **Forgemasters:** Revered artisans who specialize in repurposing salvaged tech into brutal, effective weapons and armor, entirely independent of complex software.

Their society is a meritocracy based on strength, utility, and loyalty to the Collective's survival. 

## 3. Technology & Magic
The Ironborn actively reject magic and high-level programming. Their technology is entirely mechanical or relies on simple, robust electronics. Their **Engineering** skills are focused on practical durability rather than elegance. They use heavy, kinetic weaponry, thick kinetic plating, and improvised explosive devices. In **Combat**, they are unmatched in raw ferocity and stamina. Their profound **Survival** instincts make them the only faction capable of thriving in the toxic wastelands of the grid's forgotten fringes.

## 4. Notable Figures
- **Grom The Anvil:** A massive, cybernetically enhanced (though he claims the enhancements are purely mechanical) warlord who unified three warring scrapyards into the largest Ironborn stronghold, "The Rust Citadel."
- **Kira 'Scrap-Rat':** The most successful and famous scavenger in the Collective, known for navigating anomaly storms using only analog compasses and sheer intuition.

## 5. Relationships
- **Technocrats:** Deeply distrustful. The Ironborn view the Technocrats' reliance on code as an exploitable weakness, and frequently raid their automated resource convoys.
- **Keepers of the Veil:** Disinterested hostility. They find the Keepers' reliance on magic absurd and dangerous, though they occasionally trade raw materials for the Keepers' medicinal supplies.
